name: slurp-improve
description: Use product analytics evidence to choose and prepare a product improvement or experiment, then record what was learned.
---
For goal-driven improvement work, read `references/learning-loop.md` and `slurp/GOALS.md` first. Ask only for missing priorities, save agreed goals and dated learnings there, and resume the next check on later invocations. Keep standalone queries and reports focused on the user’s request.

Read references/learning-loop.md. Inspect product context and previous decisions. Identify the important uncertainty and cheapest credible test. Read references/experiments.md when a randomized experiment is appropriate and references/benchmarks.md for external comparisons. Prepare scoped code, tests and measurement; obtain human approval before production experiments or releases. Preserve a dated decision with hypothesis, metric contract, SQL, source/checkpoint, cutoff, result, limitations and next action. Use slurp status, describe, sync and sql --file to investigate; do not claim causality from before/after changes.
